## 0.1.3

This is a prerelease cut from the branch that carries the fix, not from `main`. It
moves window chrome and the boot screen and nothing else: no reader behaviour, no
format, no preference is touched, so a library you already imported opens exactly as
it did in 0.1.2.

### The bar is grabbable again

- Every drag surface in MAReader is a container: its pixels belong to a title, a
  search field, a row of buttons. A region that claims only the element it is written
  on therefore claims nothing at all. The bar, the strip it hovers out of and the
  sidebar header's strip now claim their subtrees, so a press anywhere in them that is
  not a control moves the window, and a double-click maximizes it — on Windows and
  Linux as well as macOS.
- Controls keep their own clicks. Buttons, links, inputs, selects, text areas, labels,
  anything `contenteditable`, anything focusable, anything given an interactive role:
  a press on one belongs to that control. The library's search pill opts its whole
  subtree out on purpose, so pressing its padding focuses the field instead of sliding
  the window away from under the caret.
- A frame with no way to reach the window claims nothing. In a plain browser, or while
  a frame is still starting up, the bar behaves like any other part of the page rather
  than swallowing your selection for a drag that cannot happen.
- macOS looked fine through all of this because its native overlay title bar was
  catching the press over the top of the bar. That is why the bug belonged to one
  platform and the fix belongs to three.

### The caption knows the window's state

- An event listener registers in one frame's registry, and the window's own events —
  resize, focus — are delivered where the window lives: the shell's frame. A frame's
  listeners are now registered with that frame, whether the frame holds its own
  window surface or reaches through the shell's. This is what lets the maximize glyph
  follow the window, and on the same path it is what lets focus, shelf cover progress
  and streamed AI replies reach a Windows frame at all.
- A window that cannot be asked is no longer read as "not maximized". The probe answers
  *unknown*, and the caption declines to write a state it did not learn. The
  subscription also stopped giving up at mount time: in a route frame the surface to
  talk to arrives after the bar is built, and the first resize after that now counts.

### A start that explains itself

- Both placeholders — the markup in the page before any code runs, and the card a slow
  runtime shows in its place — carry the app's own loader mark, and the words walk a
  line: still loading the app at 4 seconds, the console names what the shell waits on
  at 9, the shell did not start at 20. The copy says what is genuinely known and stops
  there; nothing is invented to sound reassuring.
- Content comes in after 200 ms, so a handoff that lands within a frame or two stays
  still and only a wait that is real gets an explanation. With animations reduced or
  switched off you still get the mark and the words: what shortens is the fade, never
  the information.
- The launch colour is also handed to the window itself as early as a page can run,
  which is the half that could not simply be fixed here. A blank that outlives the
  first paint is the webview's own behaviour on Windows, and the only thing a page can
  choose before it paints is what sits behind it.

### Known limits

macOS builds are ad-hoc signed and there is no updater feed yet, so an install is a
manual one. No automated lane can drag a real window or press its maximize button — a
browser has no window to move — so the pinned gates assert which press posts which
command, and the caption's state, the double-click and the boot copy are checked in a
real browser. The feel of a drag on a desktop is yours to confirm.
