Plain Notes

A short plain-text document the browser suite opens as a third pane, beside
a PDF and a Markdown document, so one workspace runs three different format
sessions at once.

Why it exists

Disposing the reader must release every pane's owner, whatever the pane
shows. This file is the plain-text one: no headings, no markup, just lines.

What it contains

A few paragraphs of ordinary text, wrapped at a comfortable width, and
nothing else. It is small on purpose: the suite measures lifecycles, not
rendering.
